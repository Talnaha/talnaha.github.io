The Sounds of My Research

The cloud is built from the titles, abstracts, and key themes of my papers, with each word sized according to how central it is to the body of work. When you hover over a word (or tap it on a phone), the browser plays a short tone that relates to the word frequency: the word's weight is normalised to a value between 0 and 1, then mapped to a sound of your choice. Higher-frequency words sound higher in pitch. The sound itself is generated by one or two sine-wave oscillators; everything is synthesised live in the browser.

Thanks to Amir Bolzman and Lior Pinsky who taught me the art of sound.
